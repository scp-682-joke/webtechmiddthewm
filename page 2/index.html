ARMS

Dumbbell Bicep Curl

PREPARATION

Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with an underhand grip, keeping your arms at your sides and your elbows close to your body.

Show More

EXECUTION

  1. Raise the dumbbells until your forearms are close to your chest.
  2. Pause for a few seconds and contract your biceps.
  3. Lower the dumbbells back to the starting position and repeat.

Parallel Bar Dips

PREPARATION

Keep your body upright with your legs crossed behind you. Hold the parallel bars with your arms straight and keep your elbows close to your body.

Show More

EXECUTION

  1. Bend your elbows and lower your body until your upper arms are approximately parallel to the floor.
  2. Pause briefly, then slowly push yourself back to the starting position and repeat.

Dumbbell Shoulder Press

PREPARATION

Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder level, with your elbows bent at 90 degrees and your palms facing forward.

Show More

EXECUTION

  1. Exhale and press the dumbbells upward until your arms are directly above your head.
  2. Pause briefly, then slowly lower the dumbbells back to the starting position and repeat.

LEGS

Barbell Squat

PREPARATION

Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold the barbell behind your shoulders with your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart and palms facing forward.

Show More

EXECUTION

  1. Inhale and squat down until your thighs are parallel to the floor.
  2. Pause for a few seconds and feel the muscle contraction.
  3. Exhale, slowly push through your heels and the middle of your feet to return to the starting position and repeat.

Dumbbell Lunge

PREPARATION

Stand upright and take a step forward with one leg. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with your arms extended at your sides.

Show More

EXECUTION

  1. Inhale and lower your back knee almost to the floor, bending your front knee until your thigh is parallel to the floor.
  2. Exhale and return to the starting position.
  3. Switch to the other leg and repeat.

ABS

Crunch

PREPARATION

Lie on a bench, bend your knees and secure your feet under the pads. Place your hands behind your head.

Show More

EXECUTION

  1. Exhale and contract your abs to lift your shoulders toward your knees.
  2. Pause at the top for a few seconds, inhale, slowly return to the starting position and repeat.
