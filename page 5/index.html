About calories

The amount of energy in food or drink is measured in calories.
Your body uses this energy for everyday functions and movement, from breathing to walking and exercising.

  • An average man needs around 2,500 kcal a day.
  • An average woman needs around 2,000 kcal a day.

These are general guidelines. Your energy needs depend on factors such as age, body size and how active you are. Food labels often show energy per serving and per 100 g. Check the portion size when comparing foods.

Learn more: NHS guide to calories.
