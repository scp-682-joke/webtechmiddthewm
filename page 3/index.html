BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For example, 65 kg and 1.70 m gives BMI 22.5.

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It cannot measure body fat directly or account for muscle mass.

For general information only. Adult BMI categories apply from age 20. Younger users need an age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age assessment.
